Frequently Asked Questions

What is PicSmash?

PicSmash is a tiny app for stress relief that lets you make a "smash pack" from a photo and a short sound, then send it to a friend. Your friend opens the pack and smashes the photo for fun — no scores, no pressure, just the satisfying release.

How do I make a smash pack?

On the create screen: pick a photo, record or import a sound, then tap the button to package it. The pack is saved as a .funpack file on your device.

How do I share a pack?

Use the share action to send the .funpack file however you like — AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or saving to Files. The recipient opens the file with PicSmash to play it.

How does smashing work?

Tap anywhere to smash. Every 10th smash adds a little extra pop of satisfaction. Play for ten seconds or ten minutes — it's up to you. No scores, no timers, no pressure.

How do I report inappropriate content?

If you receive a pack with inappropriate or unwanted content, tap the flag button in the top-right corner of the playing screen and choose a reason. The pack is immediately hidden from your device and flagged for review.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Photos and recordings stay on your device unless you actively share them. PicSmash does not operate servers to store your content. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete any pack from your device, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data. You can revoke microphone access anytime in iOS Settings.
