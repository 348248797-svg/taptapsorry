Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play the game?

Tap the character on the screen to score points. The faster you tap, the higher your score!

How do I save my character?

After playing a game, you can save your character to the Character Library. Go to Character Library from the home screen to view and manage saved characters.

How do I share a character?

Open Character Library, tap the Share button on any saved character, and choose how you'd like to share it.

How do I use a custom voice?

When creating a character, select "Custom" as the voice option and import an audio file from your device.

Is my data safe?

Yes! All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, upload, or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete saved characters in the Character Library, or uninstall the app to remove all data from your device.
