PicBonk Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Overview

PicBonk (the "App") is a lightweight entertainment app that lets you make a "smash pack" from a photo and a short sound, then share it with friends to smash for fun and stress relief. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

PicBonk is designed with privacy in mind: your photos and recordings stay on your device unless you actively choose to share them. The App does not upload your media to our servers, and we do not operate a backend that stores your content.

Information the App Accesses

  • Microphone. When you record a smash sound, the App requests microphone access. Recordings are saved locally on your device and are only used inside the pack you create. You can decline microphone access and still import a sound from your files instead.
  • Photos. The App uses the system photo picker so you can choose a photo to smash. The picker is provided by iOS and only gives the App access to the photo you explicitly select. The App never browses or uploads your entire photo library.
  • User-Generated Content. The packs you create contain a photo and a sound you choose. These packs are stored locally and are only shared when you actively send them (for example, via AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or saving to Files).
  • Local Data. The App stores your reporting preferences, blocked senders, and settings locally on your device. This helps the App remember content you have hidden or blocked between sessions.

How the Information Is Used

The App uses the information above solely to provide the core experience: creating smash packs, smashing them, and moderating reported content. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

User-Generated Content & Sharing

Sharing is entirely user-initiated. A smash pack leaves your device only when you use a share action to send it. We do not automatically transmit, collect, or publish your content. Once you share a pack, the recipient can play it and may forward it further, similar to any file you send.

Content Reporting & Safety

If you receive a pack with inappropriate, offensive, or unwanted content, you can report it using the flag button inside the pack. Reporting hides the pack from your device and records it for review. To help keep the community safe, reporting also blocks further packs from the same sender on your device. If you believe your content has been used without your permission, you can report it as well.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it does not integrate with social networks or require an account. Your content and data remain on your device unless you actively share them.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Content is created and shared by the user, and we do not collect age or identity information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through misuse of the App, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Retention & Your Choices

Most data is stored only on your device. You can delete any pack, remove imported sounds, or delete the App to erase the data stored on your device. You can revoke microphone access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
