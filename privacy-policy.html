Bang Bang Sorry - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Bang Bang Sorry! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our app.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No name, email, phone number, or location data is collected
  • No account or registration is required
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are integrated
  • No crash reporting tools are used
  • No advertising SDKs are integrated

Any data within the app (such as custom avatar images or imported audio files) is provided voluntarily by the user through active interaction. The app does not automatically collect, scan, or transmit any data in the background.

Data Retention

All data is retained locally on your device until you manually delete it or uninstall the app. We do not retain any data on any server. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data.

No Tracking

Bang Bang Sorry does NOT track users across apps or websites owned by other companies. The app does not use the AppTrackingTransparency (ATT) framework, does not access the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), and does not engage in any form of cross-app or cross-website tracking.

Data Stored Locally

All app data is stored locally on your device:

  • Avatar creation data (custom images) is saved in the app sandbox
  • Imported audio files are stored in the app sandbox Documents directory
  • Saved character profiles are stored using UserDefaults
  • No data is uploaded to any server

Sharing Feature

The app includes a character sharing feature that uses the iOS system share sheet. When you choose to share a saved character, a file is generated in a temporary directory. This file is only shared with the app or service you explicitly select. We do not have access to or control over shared files once they leave your device.

Photo Library Access

The app requests access to your photo library only when you choose to create a custom avatar from your photos. This access is optional and only used to set your character image. No photos are uploaded or shared.

Children's Privacy

Bang Bang Sorry is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, our app is safe for children to use.

Third-Party Services

Bang Bang Sorry does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Deleting saved characters in the Character Library
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
